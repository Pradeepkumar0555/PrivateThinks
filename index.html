Sacred Ganesha Shloka
वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ ।
निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा ॥
"O Lord with the curved trunk and immense radiant aura equal to millions of suns, please remove every obstacle from all my noble undertakings forever."

Ashta Blessings of Bappa

May the 8 divine attributes of Lord Ganesha enrich your journey

💡
बुद्धि (Buddhi)
Supreme intellect, sharp wisdom, and sound clarity in every decision.
💎
ऋद्धि (Riddhi)
Abundant prosperity, wealth, and continuous auspicious fortune.
🎯
सिद्धि (Siddhi)
Mastery, accomplishment, and glorious success in all endeavors.
🌿
आरोग्य (Arogya)
Vibrant health, longevity, and peaceful vitality for your loved ones.
🛡️
विघ्नहर्ता (Protection)
Dissolution of hardships, worries, and unforeseen hurdles.
🕊️
शांति (Shanti)
Inner serenity, blissful home environment, and calm mindfulness.
👑
सौभाग्य (Saubhagya)
Unending good luck, respect, and honorable social prosperity.
✨
आनंद (Ananda)
Celebratory joy, harmonious relationships, and sweet memories.
